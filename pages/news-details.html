El-Hamd Exports Expands Processing Capacity in Fayoum Facility

El-Hamd Exports has officially commissioned its expanded optical sorting and steam sterilisation line at its primary Fayoum facility, increasing total processing capacity to over 5,000 metric tons per year.

The new facility expansion includes state-of-the-art Sortex color sorters, high-capacity mechanical sieving, and closed-loop dust extraction systems designed specifically for medicinal herbs such as chamomile, peppermint, and hibiscus.

With European and North American B2B buyers placing stricter food safety requirements on volatile oils, microbial counts, and heavy metal limits, El-Hamd's upgraded in-house laboratory now provides real-time gas chromatography and microbiological testing for every export batch before port dispatch.

"This investment reflects our core promise: consistent quality built on auditable processes rather than verbal assurances. Our global partners can now rely on reduced lead times and even higher purity grades across all four core product lines."

The newly expanded lines are fully integrated with the company's ISO 22000, HACCP, and EU Organic certified quality management systems.
