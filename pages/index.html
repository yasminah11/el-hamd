The Company

A grower and exporter built for long-term supply

El-Hamd Exports has farmed and processed Egyptian botanicals since 1998. We contract directly with growers across 3,000+ feddans in Fayoum, Qena and Assiut, control every processing step in our own facility, and test every lot before it ships.

Quality at El-Hamd is a process, not a promise — documented, repeatable, and auditable at every stage.

  • Own processing facility in Fayoum Agricultural Zone
  • In-house laboratory — moisture, volatiles, microbiology, pesticides
  • Export-ready documentation for all major markets
