About Us

Who We Are

Founded in 1998 as a family farm in the Fayoum basin, El-Hamd Exports has grown into one of Egypt's most trusted exporters of medicinal plants and agricultural produce. We farm and contract over 3,000 feddans, operate our own processing facility, and ship to 40+ markets worldwide.

Mission

Our Mission

To deliver clean, traceable Egyptian botanicals that manufacturers can source with confidence — season after season.

Vision

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and sustainable source of medicinal plants and agricultural produce from the Nile Valley.
